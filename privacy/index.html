Privacy overview

This portfolio website is designed as a static public information site. Product services publish their own product-specific privacy boundaries.

Portfolio website

RegEvidenceHub.com does not require an account and is intended to operate without a customer database. Standard hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request metadata required to serve the site.

Product services

Individual MCP products may record bounded operational or aggregate telemetry described by their own public privacy/support pages. They are designed not to require private keys or seed phrases.

Do not submit unnecessary sensitive data

Do not send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, payment credentials, patient records, full care files, or unrelated confidential material to a RegEvidenceHub tool.
