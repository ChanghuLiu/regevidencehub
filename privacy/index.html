Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026. This policy describes the data handling for RegEvidenceHub.com and the public RegEvidenceHub AI/MCP surfaces used for regulatory workflow navigation and preflight.

At a glance: no account is needed. Contact details are used to answer enquiries; tool inputs are used to return the requested result. Avoid sending secrets or sensitive records.

Data we process

The portfolio website does not require an account. Standard hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and security-related request information needed to serve and protect the site.

Optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) runs only after you select “Allow analytics” in the site banner. It measures page views with query strings and fragments removed. DirectHire Ready PH adds coarse milestone event names for route completion, checker start/completion and the printable-report interest click; it does not attach checklist answers, province, passport data, document contents or contact details to those custom events. The Works and Premises report flows may record product CTA clicks, report-form views/starts, checkout attempts, and when a server-verified paid report is displayed. Those funnel events contain only a fixed product ID and event name; they never include form values, applicant age, local authority, activity selections, email address, case facts, order references, checkout tokens or Stripe identifiers. Consent is shared across RegEvidenceHub subdomains. RegEvidenceHub and Agent Trust Lab share one GA4 measurement ID; events include the site_platform label and are also separated by hostname. Google processes analytics data under its service terms. You can reject analytics or change your choice at any time using “Cookie preferences” on the page.

Enquiries submitted through Support & Contact include your name, email address, optional organization, enquiry type, message, and source website. The shared commercial email service sends them to our support inbox; the enquiry content is not stored in the website database. The email is retained in the support mailbox under that provider's retention settings. Do not include confidential, regulated, or sensitive personal information. You can also contact selena@regevidencehub.com.

Public AI/MCP tools process the task-specific facts supplied in a tool call so they can return the requested workflow guidance or regulatory preflight. Depending on the product and client, bounded operational telemetry may include timestamp, tool name, success or error outcome, latency, declared client or integration name when supplied, coarse source attribution, request or correlation identifier when supplied, and deployment revision.

Data we do not need

Do not submit passwords, private keys, seed phrases, payment credentials, patient records, full care files, full conversation histories, contact lists, or unrelated confidential information. RegEvidenceHub public AI/MCP surfaces are designed to work from bounded task facts rather than broad personal profiles.

How we use information

Information is used to provide the requested tool result, maintain service reliability, diagnose failures, protect the services from abuse, monitor official-source health, and measure aggregate product usage. We do not use public AI/MCP tool usage to build advertising profiles.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure and hosting providers used to deliver and secure the website and MCP services. We do not sell user data. We do not share tool inputs with regulators or licensing authorities unless a separate product feature explicitly says it performs such an action; the current public RegEvidenceHub OpenAI submission surfaces do not submit anything to regulators.

Retention

The RegEvidenceHub portfolio website does not intentionally maintain an application-level customer database. Hosting providers may retain infrastructure and security logs according to their own service settings and policies.

The public OpenAI submission surfaces are designed not to persist substantive regulatory case payloads at the application layer. Where other RegEvidenceHub product surfaces retain bounded operational telemetry for reliability, security, attribution, or aggregate measurement, there is currently no guaranteed automatic deletion interval; operational records may be retained until they are no longer reasonably needed for those purposes or are deleted in response to a valid request.

User controls

Because the public surfaces do not require user accounts, there is no account dashboard containing personal data. You may ask about access, correction, or deletion of information that can reasonably be identified as relating to your use by emailing selena@regevidencehub.com. Include only the minimum information needed to locate the record, such as approximate date/time, product used, and a request/correlation identifier if one was shown. Do not send confidential case documents.

International processing

Service infrastructure may process request data in countries other than your own, subject to the safeguards and terms of the infrastructure providers used to operate the service.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the services change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy questions may be sent to selena@regevidencehub.com.
