About RegEvidenceHub

Independent tools for evidence-led checks.

RegEvidenceHub is the customer-facing product platform of Technologies RegEvidence Inc. It brings together focused software for evidence-led UK regulatory preparation and industrial quality checks. AgentTrustLab remains a separate platform brand in the wider company portfolio.

Each product has a defined scope and explains when information is missing or needs human review. Product-line grouping does not imply regulator approval, certification, or independent assessment.

Independent service: RegEvidenceHub is not a government department, regulator, council, standards body, manufacturer, or licensing authority. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by those organizations, and it cannot issue their decisions, approvals, or certifications. For regulatory requirements, the relevant authority’s own published material remains the primary source.

Eight products across two product lines

UK Regulatory Readiness contains five scoped regulatory-preparation tools. Industrial Quality Assurance contains three file, evidence, and model-compatibility checks. These summaries describe scope only; they are not official requirements or approval statements.

Taxi and private hire

UK Taxi PHV Licensing

Preflight information for supported taxi and private-hire licensing authorities, with missing facts and authority sources kept visible.

Health and social care

CQC Provider Compliance

Workflow navigation and evidence checks for England provider registration, changes, and statutory notifications.

Premises licensing

UK Premises Licence Preflight

Preparation checks for supported Licensing Act 2003 authorities, including local-policy coverage and missing information.

Sponsor compliance

England Works Watch

Evidence-linked checks for defined UK Skilled Worker sponsor-change events using GOV.UK sources.

Waste compliance

RegEvidenceHub Waste

England waste workflow checks for registration, Digital Waste Tracking readiness, permit changes, and official-source status.

Structured cabling

CableCert QA

Compares user-provided cable inventories and contractor CSV evidence against stated expectations and test limits.

Industrial imports

FactoryTalk Import Preflight

Checks user-provided FactoryTalk View tag and alarm CSV files against documented import-layout rules. It is independent of Rockwell Automation.

OPC UA information models

NodeSet Compatibility Gate

Compares user-provided OPC UA NodeSet2 XML revisions for documented structural changes. It is independent of the OPC Foundation.

Related platform: AgentTrustLab

AgentTrustLab provides a separate evaluation and trust-review platform for AI tools and MCP selection evidence. Its evaluation signals are distinct from product availability, regulatory outcomes, and third-party certification.

How to read a result

Regulatory tools should identify the official source behind a requirement and distinguish source status from the result for the facts supplied. Industrial tools report checks against user-provided files and documented rules; they do not establish safety, certification, or manufacturer acceptance. Missing or conflicting information should remain visible instead of being guessed.
