Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

This policy applies to USB OTG Checker for Android, package com.usbotg.checker, provided by Technologies RegEvidence inc.

In brief: USB OTG Checker has no user account or developer-operated app server. USB detection and history are processed on your device. Version 0.5.0 (code 5) includes Google Mobile Ads and UMP libraries, but its Release configuration does not initialize Mobile Ads or request ads. Future ad-enabled versions may process additional data through Google as described below.

Information processed on your device

Android's USB Host APIs provide the phone's host-feature declaration, connected USB device descriptors, and per-device permission and connection results. The app uses this information for its check, device details, troubleshooting, input-test guidance, and optional local history. The app does not send USB hardware details or history to the developer. It does not request or read USB serial numbers and does not read files from USB storage. Keyboard, mouse, and game-controller input checks run in the foreground; input values are not saved in history.

Detection and connection records are stored in app-private local storage until you delete them or remove the app's data. A PDF is generated only when you request export and select a destination in Android's document picker. A text report is shared only after you choose a destination in Android's share sheet. The storage or sharing provider you select may process exported content under its own terms.

The app has no account, analytics, crash-reporting service, or developer-operated application server. USB information and local history are not uploaded by the app.

Google advertising and consent libraries

Release 0.5.0 (version code 5) packages Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK 1.7.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. In this Release build, ad requests are disabled, the Mobile Ads SDK is not initialized, the AdMob application ID is omitted, and the app does not request location permission. The Release code returns before making a UMP consent-info request. No ads are served by this build.

If a later version enables advertising, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share the device IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions such as launches, taps, and video views, diagnostics such as crash and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. SDK behavior and data may change by version, configuration, Android settings, region, and privacy choices. UMP may present consent or privacy-option messages where required. The Play Data Safety disclosure must be updated for any ad-enabled release.

Google's current information: GMA Next-Gen SDK Play data disclosure, GMA Next-Gen release notes, UMP privacy and consent, and Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising is optional and does not unlock or restrict checks, troubleshooting, history, or reports. Where enabled, a rewarded ad may hide the banner for one hour; it does not unlock a feature.

Retention, deletion, and children

Local records remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove app data or uninstall the app. The developer does not receive USB records. Google handles any data collected through its services under Google's policies. The app is a general utility and is not designed specifically for children; the final Play target-audience declaration is managed separately in Play Console.

Changes and contact

This policy will be reviewed when the app, included SDKs, or advertising configuration changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published.
