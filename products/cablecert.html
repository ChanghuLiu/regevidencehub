Free first step

Use the free MCP service-information and CSV-inspection tools to see detected fields. They do not reconcile the owner inventory against the contractor evidence. A paid one-project browser report is available below.

Input: CSV only, up to 5 MB, plus the owner-supplied expected link inventory.

Paid audit options

  • MCP: $0.01 USDC per completed audit.
  • Project report: $19 USD for one project audit. The entitlement expires after 24 hours and does not renew.

Use the report form to submit the expected Cable IDs and contractor CSV. The shared account page will show the offer, ask you to sign in, and send you to Stripe only after you confirm checkout.

Prepare a one-project report

What it checks

  • Expected versus submitted Cable IDs
  • Missing and unexpected evidence
  • Duplicate and retest lineage
  • Owner-supplied test-limit mismatches
  • Source file and row provenance

Decision boundary

CableCert reports evidence exceptions. It does not certify cabling, determine TIA compliance or safety, or accept contractor work. A qualified human reviewer decides whether the package meets project requirements.

Ordinary MCP checks process CSV in memory and are not intentionally persisted. If you opt into the paid browser report, only normalized audit fields are kept for up to 24 hours to restore the case; raw CSV bytes and unmapped columns are not retained. Read the product privacy notice before submitting project evidence.
