Charging Checker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy applies to Charging Checker for Android, package com.charging.checker, provided by Technologies RegEvidence Inc.

In brief: Charging Checker has no user account or developer-operated app server. Battery readings, saved tests and generated reports are processed on your device. Google Mobile Ads and Google consent services process data as described below.

Information processed on your device

While you use the app, Android provides battery current, voltage, temperature, charge level, charge state and connection type. The app uses those values to display readings, conditionally calculate a clearly labelled battery-side estimate, and create a stability-test result. The app does not send these measurements, your charger or cable labels, or your saved test history to the developer.

Saved tests are stored locally. A PDF is created only when you choose to export one. The Android document picker lets you select its destination; a destination may be local or a cloud storage provider you selected. The app does not request registration, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access.

Advertising and consent

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may process device or other identifiers, IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions such as ad views or taps, and diagnostic information to provide, measure and protect advertising, including analytics and fraud prevention. Google describes Mobile Ads SDK data as encrypted in transit. Exact processing may depend on your device settings, region, consent choices and enabled ad features.

Where required, UMP presents choices before an ad request. You can open available privacy options in the app's Settings. Optional ads support the app; all detection features and PDF reports are free. An ad does not cover or interrupt a running charging test. A rewarded ad can hide the banner temporarily; it does not unlock or restrict any feature.

Google's information: Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and UMP privacy and consent.

Sharing, retention and deletion

The developer does not receive app measurements or local history. Google may receive information through its SDKs as described above. Saved tests remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app's data or uninstall it. A PDF saved to a location you select is then managed by that storage provider.

Children

Charging Checker is a general utility and is not designed for children. Advertising and consent processing are subject to Google's applicable policies and the choices available in your region.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its SDK configuration changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published.
