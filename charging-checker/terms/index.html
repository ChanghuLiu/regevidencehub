What the app does

Charging Checker reads battery information supplied by Android and presents live readings, charging trends, stability-test summaries, locally saved history, comparisons and user-requested PDF reports. Sensor availability, sampling, units, accuracy and current sign conventions vary by device, manufacturer, Android version, battery level and operating conditions.

Measurement limitation: “Estimated Battery Power” is an estimate on the phone's battery side. It is not a measurement of the charger's actual output. The app does not electrically test a USB cable or charger. Labels you enter for a charger or cable are organizational labels only.

Measurements are informational and are not a safety certification, electrical inspection, repair diagnosis or guarantee of charging performance. Do not rely on the app to determine whether a charger, cable, battery or device is safe. Stop using damaged or overheating equipment and follow the device manufacturer's safety instructions.
