OEC for Direct Hire Canada: what comes before it

For a new Direct Hire case, OEC is not the first checkpoint. The route typically involves verified employment documents, DMW Direct Hire evaluation and clearance before later-stage departure requirements.

MWO verification matters

MWO Vancouver states that verification of required employment documents is a requisite for DMW registration and OEC processing for recruited or hired Filipino workers in its jurisdiction.

Direct Hire Clearance is separate

DMW regional guidance describes Direct Hire applicants uploading requirements to POPS-Direct and waiting for evaluation and issuance of Direct Hire Clearance.

Use the checker for readiness, not issuance

DirectHire Ready PH checks preparation against a versioned rule pack. It does not issue an OEC and does not represent DMW or an MWO.

Scope boundary. This is a pre-submission readiness tool, not DMW/MWO approval, legal advice, Direct Hire Clearance, or OEC issuance. When current official sources conflict or do not verify a requirement, the checker returns REVIEW rather than guessing.
