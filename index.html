Why RegEvidenceHub

Bounded checks, with evidence behind each result

Regulatory products organize official guidance and local policy into reviewed decision logic. Industrial tools compare caller-supplied files against defined input contracts. Both report what was checked and hold back conclusions when facts or evidence are not sufficient.

Evidence-linked

Results retain relevant source provenance instead of returning unsupported conclusions.

Deterministic

Core regulatory checks use structured rules rather than asking a language model to invent an answer.

Freshness-aware

Decision-bearing sources are monitored for change, availability, and unresolved conflicts.

Fail closed

When evidence cannot safely support a conclusion, the result is withheld or routed to review.

How it works

Built for people, businesses and AI workflows

1

Supply facts

A person, business workflow or AI agent provides structured facts relevant to the regulatory question.

2

Select rules

The product applies the relevant reviewed regulatory rules for its bounded scope.

3

Check evidence

Source status and evidence dependencies are evaluated before a decision is relied on.

4

Return a result

The service returns a structured result, evidence context, and next action or review state.

The deterministic decision path does not require a server-side LLM.
