Pricing

Web reports and MCP tools have separate prices

Taxi, CQC and Waste have a free human web readiness step before an optional paid report. Premises and Works web forms start on the paid report path. Free MCP information or readiness tools are separate from web reports and do not include the full paid decision or audit.
