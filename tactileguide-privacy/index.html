TactileGuide for Android

Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Technologies RegEvidence Inc. handles information in the TactileGuide Android app.

About TactileGuide

TactileGuide is an accessibility-focused prototype for preparing and reviewing step-by-step instructions from selected PDF and image documents. The current Play test build does not have an analysis server connected, so imported documents are not sent to a TactileGuide server and real document analysis is unavailable in this build.

Information handled on your device

When you choose a PDF or image, Android grants TactileGuide access to the selected file. The app processes the selected material on your device. It may keep the selected source reference, file name, import details, extracted text, prepared page images, and related instruction or evidence records in the app's private storage so the app can display and continue the workflow.

If you choose to capture a camera image during a recovery workflow, the image and its associated evidence record are stored in the app's private storage. Camera capture is optional and requires your explicit action. The app provides a control to delete a captured camera evidence image.

TactileGuide does not require an account and this build has no advertising, in-app purchases, or developer-operated analytics. The app does not send imported documents, extracted text, camera images, or tactile observations to the developer in this build.

On-device text recognition and Google ML Kit

TactileGuide uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to recognize text from document page images on the device. The document text-recognition feature processes the image locally. Google may receive separate SDK diagnostics and performance telemetry, such as app and device information, device or installation identifiers, SDK or API usage events, performance measurements, and error information. This telemetry is used by Google to operate, diagnose, and improve its SDKs. It is transmitted over an encrypted connection. TactileGuide does not use this telemetry for advertising.

Google handles SDK telemetry under its own service terms and privacy disclosures. See Google ML Kit terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and service providers

Technologies RegEvidence Inc. does not sell TactileGuide user information. The current app build does not share selected documents or their contents with the developer or other companies. Google ML Kit may collect the SDK telemetry described above for its service operation. Android and Google Play also process information under their own services and policies.

Retention and deletion

App-created document records, prepared page images, and observations remain in TactileGuide's private storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To clear this information, use Android Settings > Apps > TactileGuide > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall TactileGuide. Camera evidence can also be deleted from its recovery screen using the in-app delete control. Because documents are not sent to the developer in this build, the developer does not hold a separate copy to delete on your behalf. Google controls retention of its SDK telemetry under its own policies.

Security

App documents and records are kept in storage private to TactileGuide on your device. The app does not claim that locally stored files are encrypted. Google ML Kit telemetry sent off the device is transmitted over an encrypted connection. Keep your Android device protected and clear app storage when you no longer need locally stored test documents.

Children

TactileGuide is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. We will post the current version at this URL and update the effective date.
