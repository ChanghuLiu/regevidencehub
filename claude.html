Claude · three industrial MCP tools

Connect Claude to our industrial tools

Choose one product, copy its URL, and enable it in a Claude conversation.

Three steps

  1. Choose a product below and copy its URL.
  2. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors → + Add → Add custom connector. Enter the product name and paste its URL, then add it.
  3. In a chat, open + → Connectors, turn it on, and send its First test prompt.
Before an audit: connecting does not run or pay for one. Check the product details for inputs and payment terms. The setup needs a name and URL; do not paste JSON or wallet secrets.
